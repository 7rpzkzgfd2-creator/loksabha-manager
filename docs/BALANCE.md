## Questions the batch was asked

**Does wealth dominate?** INC with four times the money averaged 69.3 seats against 65.2 at normal funding (+4.0). Money is capped by leader days and team capacity, so a richer party cannot simply buy every seat.

**Do alliances always help? (NDA)** Members won 311.4 seats together with the alliance and 300.5 without it. No member did clearly worse inside it.

**Do alliances always help? (INDIA)** Members won 155.0 seats together with the alliance and 142.8 without it. Members who did worse inside the alliance: INC, RJD, AAP.

**Does rally spam beat strategy?** Scripted INC strategies at normal difficulty: doing nothing 53.9 seats, leader rallies hammered into the same three safe seats 51.7, targeted town halls and door-to-door in toss-ups 59.4.

**Are incumbents unbeatable?** In AI-only normal runs, 77% of seats stayed with the party that won them in the (simulated) previous contest. Real Lok Sabha seat retention varies a lot between elections; anything far above about 75% would suggest the model is too sticky.

**Do preferences move too easily?** The largest one-week change in any party's projected seats averaged 17.5 (max 38).

**Votes vs seats.** The party with the most votes failed to win the most seats in 0 of 500 runs. Mean final-forecast seat error (half the sum of absolute errors) was 32.6 seats.

**Can small parties grow?** A new custom party based in Maharashtra won 0.0 seats on average (best 0, 0/40 runs with at least one seat), on 0.9% of the national vote.

**Does difficulty work through AI behaviour?** The same scripted INC strategy won 57.3 seats on easy, 59.4 on normal and 54.4 on hard. The vote arithmetic is identical across difficulties; only rival decision quality and the player's starting money and gaffe and crisis forgiveness differ.

**Does dirty money pay?** Taking ₹25 cr off the books every week: 39.5 seats against 59.4 for the clean version of the same strategy; caught in 37 of 40 runs.

## Plausibility checks

- Government formed by (normal AI-only): BJP 49, INC 1.
- Mean turnout 65.4%.
- 2019 scenario: BJP 325 seats on average (recorded: 303). 2014 scenario: BJP 289 (recorded: 282). Starting shares are rough estimates, so these are sanity checks, not calibration targets.
